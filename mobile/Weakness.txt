Weaknesses you WILL be asked about — prepare answers:

"How accurate is your signal strength?"
You can't get real RSRP in Expo managed workflow, so you use latency → dBm proxy. Be upfront: it's a relative quality indicator, not a calibrated measurement. The 5-tier thresholds are consistent across the system. A bare-workflow native module could give real RSRP — that's a future improvement.

"iOS doesn't return the carrier — how does that affect your data?"
Android is the primary target. iOS data enters the DB as "iOS Carrier" and is excluded from per-provider analysis. All 4 Nigerian carriers (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile) are Android-sourced. This is the same constraint NCC-licensed tools face.

"Your heatmap might be sparse — how do you handle low data density?"
The geohash precision-6 cells (~1.2 km²) aggregate all readings in that area. Sparse areas simply show no circles — which is itself valid information (no coverage data collected there yet). This is honest rather than extrapolating.

"Why not authenticate users?"
Crowdsourcing works better with low friction. Anonymous device IDs (UUID stored in AsyncStorage) allow per-device history tracking without registration barriers. Trust comes from volume and median aggregation, not identity.

"Background collection on iOS is minimum 15 minutes, not 10"
iOS BGFetch has a system-controlled minimum. Your 10-min setting is honoured on Android; iOS respects it as a request but enforces its own minimum. Documented limitation.
